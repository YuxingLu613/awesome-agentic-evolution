# Call for Contributors

Awesome Agentic Evolution is building a high-signal, community-maintained map
of agents that improve through experience, feedback, self-modification, or
co-evolution.

We welcome researchers, engineers, open-source maintainers, students, and
technical writers who want to help make this emerging area easier to
understand and evaluate.

## Ways to Contribute

- Suggest a paper, repository, benchmark, article, or technical discussion.
- Verify an existing entry against its primary sources.
- Reproduce a result or document a failure mode.
- Review safety, regression, rollback, or reward-hacking evidence.
- Curate a focused section such as memory, skills, workflows, environments,
  code evolution, or multi-agent co-evolution.
- Write a monthly synthesis or help build a reproducible tutorial demo.

Good first contributions are intentionally small. One carefully verified
resource is more valuable than a long unfiltered list.

## How to Start

1. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and
   [COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md).
2. Open a
   [resource suggestion](https://github.com/YuxingLu613/awesome-agentic-evolution/issues/new?template=resource.yml)
   or submit a focused pull request.
3. Explain what evolves, what feedback drives the update, what persists, and
   how the claimed improvement is evaluated.
4. Disclose whether you are an author, maintainer, employee, or otherwise
   connected to the submitted work.

## Become a Curator or Reviewer

Founding curator and reviewer roles are open. Curators maintain a narrow
section; reviewers focus on evidence, reproducibility, or safety. See
[COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md) for expectations and open areas.

## Recognition and Future Publications

Repository contributions are recorded and credited publicly. Resource
submissions alone do not automatically establish authorship, but sustained,
substantive work can qualify contributors for survey-paper authorship. Eligible
work includes evidence curation, reproducibility work, section leadership,
cross-category analysis, synthesis, writing, software, visualization, and
revision. Contributor status is not a barrier: decisions will use the public
contribution record and CRediT-style roles rather than invitation or maintainer
status.
